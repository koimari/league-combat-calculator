| SR8 | cast pricing | 7 options state a STACK LEVEL the fight builds and a cast reads; `docs/coverage-status.md` lists them and marks which way each errs. Case 6 is the shape for the CONSUMING sub-shape and Tristana, Yasuo, Varus, Mel and Kalista are on it; a rule states which side of the cast its window sits on, because a charge the cast attaches collects hits after it and a level the cast consumes reads hits before it. The RAMP sub-shape has its engine, both of its streams, and a way for one count to feed a second mechanic. A module publishes a `swing_ramp`, `stat_buff_ultimates._kit_swing_ramp` resolves it into a `KitRamp` naming which streams stack it, and `rearmed_swings.swing_times` adds its bonus to a build ramp's rather than letting one overwrite the other. `DecayingStackRamp` prices a first stack apart where a cache does (Jinx). A ramp is stacked by the swings it re-rates (Jax, Jinx), by the ability casts (Ezreal), or by both (Volibear, Irelia). The ordering that once blocked the cast stream is gone: the rotation's cast schedule reads exactly two numbers off the swing stream, both Navori's, so `CooldownRefunds` names them and setup asks with `NO_REFUNDS`, which places every recast at or later than the fight will and makes the stack count a floor. A count that also gates a second mechanic declares `armed_procs` beside the ramp, and `retained_at_threshold` is the arm for a state the swing READS rather than a charge it spends (Volibear's Lightning Claws). Three blockers hold the rest. ONE, Zaahen's Determination grants bonus AD per stack and DOUBLES the bonus at its twelfth, which is not attack speed, so the swing ramp has no seam for it. TWO, Graves and Wukong ramp RESISTANCES, which is the same missing seam. The three want one thing: a derived stack level that reaches a stat other than attack speed. THREE, Kindred's pounce is all-or-nothing at three stacks, so a fight that stacks too little prices a zero-damage cast, and `fight/items/muramana.py` then finds no positive authored event to time its proc against and falls back to a coarse cast boundary. Kalista's floor of one avoided that; Kindred has no floor to stand on, and the honest fix is for a zero-damage ability HIT to author a timing event that `_ordered_damage_events` currently drops. Hecarim's Q is its own case: its stacks also shorten its own cooldown, so a derived level and a derived cadence decide each other. Ashe is not a ramp at all: Focus stacks are spent to cast Ranger's Focus, which is the CONSUMING shape Case 6 already covers. One question the shape has not answered: the walker seeds a swing-stacked ramp with a stack live at `t=0`, so the first swing is already rated at one stack where the cache says the attack GENERATES it. That is the item ramp's own convention, inherited rather than chosen, and moving it would move every Rageblade number. | Give a derived stack level a seam for a NON attack-speed grant, which unblocks Zaahen, Graves and Wukong together; decide whether a zero-damage hit may author a timing event before Kindred; settle the `t=0` seed on its own. |
