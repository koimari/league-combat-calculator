| SR8 | cast pricing | 15 options state a STACK LEVEL the fight builds and a cast reads; `docs/coverage-status.md` lists them and marks which way each errs. Case 6 landed the shape for the CONSUMING sub-shape (a part declares `stack_scaled_damage(level)`, the level rides the per-cast `CastPricing` seam, the rotation walks it from the streams a module declares) and Tristana, Yasuo, Varus and Mel are on it. The RAMP sub-shape is harder than it reads: Jax, Volibear, Ezreal, Ashe, Irelia, Jinx, Graves, Zaahen, Wukong and Kindred's W all apply their grant to `ctx.stats` AT PARSE, so every other slot parses against the declared level and a post-rotation rating would double count; an attack-speed ramp additionally feeds the swing stream that stacks it. A measured time-weighted mean is easy (a walk over the hit stream) and was built and reverted unused, because no champion can take it until its grant moves off the parse. Kindred's E is blocked differently: deriving her pounce makes a fight that lands fewer than three attacks price a zero-damage E cast, and the census then reports Muramana and Eclipse coarse for want of damage to attribute. | Move each ramp's grant off the parse onto a declaration the fight rates, one champion at a time; decide the zero-damage-cast attribution question before Kindred; certify `ULTIMATE_RECASTS` before Kassadin. A THIRD blocker surfaced while landing the consuming sub-shape and it is the model's own cast order: a detonation that consumes what is already on the target is cast at t=0, before the basic attacks that stack it, so Varus' Blight derives to zero in a standard rotation and Kalista's Rend derives to one. The count is right and the order is what is wrong; `cast_dependency` is where a kit says its stacking hits come first. |
