Scryglass

See exactly what a build does before you click it.

Scryglass is a research-grade League of Legends build calculator: full fight simulations with event-ordered damage, healing, shields, and cooldowns — every number sourced, labelled, and ready to compare.

How it works

1

Pick your champion and build

Choose items, boots, role quest state, and any champion options — with full item and ability sources behind every choice.

2

Set the fight

Add enemies and allies, pick a level and role, then choose the fight window: duration, rotations, and auto-attack uptime.

3

Compare builds

Score builds by total damage dealt, read the per-ability breakdown, and see exactly which mechanics are modelled vs estimated.

What you can check

Every result carries trust labels: which abilities are sourced formulas, which use a player-controlled assumption, and which mechanics are deliberately not priced. The model fails closed — unmodelled mechanics are named, never silently zeroed.
