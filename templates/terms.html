Terms of use — closed beta

Scryglass is a research tool in closed beta. By signing in with an invite code and using the calculator, you agree to these terms. They are short on purpose; if something is unclear, ask the Scryglass team.

Beta access

  • Access is limited to invited research accounts and may change or end at any time.
  • The beta may be taken offline, updated, or reset without notice while we validate the calculator.
  • Saved builds, share links, and validation feedback are beta data — do not rely on them as a permanent record.

Invite-code responsibility

  • Invite codes are personal. Do not share, resell, or publish them.
  • Each code is tied to the research account that used it; misuse of a code may revoke access for everyone on that batch.
  • If you believe your code was compromised, tell the Scryglass team so the batch can be rotated.

No warranty on calculations

  • Calculator output is a research estimate: numbers can be wrong, incomplete, or based on assumptions that do not match your game.
  • Nothing on Scryglass is advice about how to play League of Legends, buy items, or build champions.
  • Scryglass is provided "as is" without warranty of any kind, and the team is not liable for decisions made from its output.

Acceptable use

  • Use the calculator for analysis and research, not for automated abuse (scraping, load flooding, or bypassing its rate limits).
  • Do not use Scryglass to circumvent Riot's own restrictions or to build tools that give an unfair in-game advantage.
  • Do not misrepresent Scryglass output as official Riot data, or as endorsed by Riot Games.
  • We may revoke beta access for conduct that harms the service or other beta users.
