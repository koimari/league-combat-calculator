Privacy during the closed beta

Scryglass is a research tool in closed beta. This page describes what the beta stores and why. It is intentionally short; if something is missing, ask the Scryglass team directly.

What we store

  • A signed session cookie after you sign in, holding your research-account name, the invite code you used, and a seven-day expiry. It is signed, not encrypted: it contains no password.
  • Builds and share links you explicitly save, in the managed database, so a share link can restore a scenario.
  • Validation feedback you submit through the calculator's feedback flow, used to drive the champion-module validation backlog.
  • Anonymous cache and rate-limit bookkeeping (hit/miss counters, request budgets) needed to keep the calculator responsive.

What we do not do

  • No ads, no analytics beacons, no third-party trackers.
  • No Riot account login — your Riot account is never touched.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.
  • Calculation requests are never stored unless you save a build or submit feedback.

Retention and deletion

Session cookies expire after seven days. Saved builds and feedback are kept for the duration of the beta and can be deleted on request by contacting the Scryglass team. Invite codes are rotated between beta batches and do not identify individuals.
