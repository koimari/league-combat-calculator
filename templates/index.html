Theory: the unconstrained comparison state. Builds are scored exactly as you assemble them, with no check that the gold, timing, or purchase order they would require is achievable in a real game. Snapshot lens: reads the scenario as one moment in a game.     First run · about a minute

    Welcome to Scryglass

    1

    Set the scenario in the rail

    Two numbered steps on the left: Champion and Roster. A step opens in place and the duel stays live behind it, so you always see what your edit changed. Constraints — gold, objective, window, game state — ride the banner above the duel.

    Builds are not a rail step: you assemble them on the duel panel itself.

    2

    Build and read the duel

    The strip along the top answers first: Build A, the delta, Build B. Below it the two builds mirror around a delta spine — each bar reads Build B against Build A, green ahead and red behind — and every slot row on either side is a button, so you fill and tune a build right where you read it.

    3

    Open the receipts

    Open event ledger under the timeline expands the timestamped events, damage breakdown and defense receipts. Every number carries a certainty chip:

    EXACT ESTIMATE BOUNDARY

    Exact = sourced formula · Estimate = defaulted player option · Boundary = documented but not computed.
