# Manrope / Black-White Prototype

## Direction

This prototype keeps the live calculator's scenario-building flow but replaces its red-on-black diagnostic surface with a sparse comparison board. Warm ivory and deep forest are the working surfaces; the colour-rich Rift atmosphere is visible through every panel. Near-black is reserved for the result and active decisions.

## Typography

Manrope is the only typeface. Weight carries hierarchy: 500 for reading text, 600 for labels, 700 for section headings, and 800 for champion names, scores, and decisions. There is no monospace “technical” layer.

## Interaction

Champion, role, game state, objective, level, quest, boots, item slots, roster membership, rotations, uptime, and event-ledger disclosure are all interactive. The result updates immediately and remains compact until proof is requested.

## Color and focus

The palette is warm ivory, deep forest, teal-green linework, and living emerald/cobalt/violet from the Rift plate. Build A and Build B are differentiated by placement, weight, labels, and inversion rather than hue. The result card is the single dark slab; translucent tinted surfaces and a short forest rule establish focus elsewhere.

## Responsive behavior

The builder and result become a single readable column below 800px. The result remains first after the builder, while controls collapse into shorter groups. Reduced motion disables card lift transitions.

## Environment plate

`assets/rift-illustration-blender-4k.webp` is the product background: a custom illustrated Rift vista mastered as a 3840×2160 Blender image-plane render from a model-super-resolved source. It is downsampled cleanly on 1920×1080 displays; the browser never enlarges a small plate. The earlier native Blender blockout and 1600×1000 vista remain only as experiments and are not part of the product surface. Sections use translucent tinted surfaces with backdrop blur so the map remains present while text stays legible.
